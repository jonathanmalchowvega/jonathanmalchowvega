## Jonathan Malchow Vega

Knowledge organization, metadata and taxonomy. MSLIS, Simmons University.

I work on how things get described — controlled vocabularies, metadata schemas,
taxonomies and ontologies — and on the gap between what something is called and
what it actually is. Most retrieval problems turn out to be description problems
wearing a technical disguise.

**Current work**

- Designing an ontology and SKOS controlled vocabulary for AI-governance role
  description: separating advertised job titles from normalized functional roles,
  and grounding classification in duties, competencies and governance artifacts.
- Research collaborator with the Simmons HCI and data-visualization group,
  maintaining a multi-axis classification framework across a 216-paper corpus.
